About Us

Crafting Iconic
Moments Since Day One

Obsidian Global Events is a boutique luxury event and marketing agency based in the heart of Dubai. We design, produce, and execute high-impact events that leave lasting impressions — from intimate executive gatherings to large-scale international conferences and product launches.

Our team blends creative vision with flawless operational execution, drawing on years of experience across corporate events, luxury lifestyle, brand activations, and cultural experiences. With Dubai as our home and the world as our stage, we bring global standards to every project we touch.
